Where does the release time come from?

MacRumors' 13 September 2026 report ↗ estimates a rollout around 10 a.m. Pacific on Monday, 14 September. We use that single instant, 17:00 UTC, and calculate each city's local date and time, including daylight saving.

The exact hour is not guaranteed. The schedule was checked on 13 September 2026; it is not automatically updated from the article.

What happens when the countdown reaches zero?

The page changes to "Expected release time reached". That does not mean the update is available on your device. Check Settings → General → Software Update on your iPhone. Availability and download speed may vary.

How do the live clocks work?

Clocks refresh every second while this page is active, using your device's clock and time zone database. Keep automatic date and time enabled for accuracy. The countdown recalculates when you return to the page.

"Use my zone" reads your device's time zone, not your GPS location. Favourites, time format and theme are saved only in this browser. No account is needed.
